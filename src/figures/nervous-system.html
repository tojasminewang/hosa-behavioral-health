Nervous system
Central (CNS)Brain and spinal cord, encased in bone
Peripheral (PNS)Nerves carrying sensory and motor information to and from the body
SomaticSenses in, commands out to the muscles: voluntary movement
AutonomicGlands, organs and smooth muscles: the body’s “cruise control”
Sym­patheticArousal: fight-or-flight
Para­sympatheticRest, repair, energy storage
EntericNerve cells in the gut: a “second brain”
