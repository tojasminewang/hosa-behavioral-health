Signs of an opioid overdosePin-point pupils · bluish or purple lips, nose or fingernails · extremely pale or clammy face · limp body · vomiting or gurgling · cannot be awakened or speak · slow or stopped breathing or heartbeat
1 · Call 911Right away
2 · Begin CPRIf breathing has stopped or is very weak
3 · Give naloxone or nalmefeneIf available: nasal spray or injection
4 · Get medical helpThese medicines wear off and cause withdrawal
