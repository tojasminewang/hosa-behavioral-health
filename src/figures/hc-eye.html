1Cornea: clear front surface; starts bending light toward the retina2Iris: coloured muscle ring that adjusts the pupil3Pupil: the opening formed by the iris4Lens: bends light to the retina; changes shape to focus near or far (accommodation)5Retina: photoreceptor layers that transduce light6Fovea: detailed central vision; mostly cones7Optic disk: where the optic nerve exits; no rods or cones, so a blind spot8Optic nerve: leaves the eye at the optic disk
