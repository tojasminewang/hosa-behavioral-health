Hunger cues
ExternalTime of day; sight and smell of favourite foods; social setting
InternalA real nutrient shortage: stomach contractions, low blood sugar (glucose), low stored fat
Hunger hormonesGhrelin and orexins
Satiety: feeling full
Ventromedial hypothalamus (VMH)Senses satiety from hormones and other signals
Cholecystokinin (CCK)Digestive hormone released when food arrives
Leptin, a hormone from fat cells, keeps stored fat at a suitable level.
