N-REM sleep · theta and delta waves, deep physical relaxation
Stage 1Falling asleep; so brief that people deny sleeping; theta waves
Stage 2Heart rate and muscle tension drop; K complexes and sleep spindles
Stages 3–4Deep sleep with delta waves; very disoriented if woken
REM sleep · also called paradoxical sleep
REMWaking-like EEG, rapid eye movements, muscle paralysis, sympathetic activation. Starts about 90 to 120 minutes after falling asleep.
Sleep talking happens in stages 1–2 and sleepwalking in stages 3–4. A night’s sleep mixes N-REM and REM.
