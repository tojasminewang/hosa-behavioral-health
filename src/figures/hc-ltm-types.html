Long-term memory
Declarative (explicit)Conscious; easy to put into words
SemanticGeneral knowledge (what a churro is)
EpisodicPersonal experiences (a beach vacation)
Nondeclarative (implicit)Unconscious, effortless; hard to put into words
ProceduralSkilled movements
PrimingA changed response after an earlier stimulus
Classical condi­tioning
Autobiographical memories are semantic or episodic memories about yourself. Semantic, episodic and autobiographical memories involve the cerebral cortex; procedural memories, the basal ganglia.
