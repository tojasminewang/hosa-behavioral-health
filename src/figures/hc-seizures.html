SeizureSudden abnormal brain activation
PartialStarts in one area (the focal area); often has an aura; temporal-lobe seizures can cause déjà vu
GeneralizedAbnormal activation of cortex–thalamus circuits; no single starting area; no aura
Two more kinds in the slides
Tonic-clonicLoss of consciousness, stopped breathing, intense contraction, violent rhythmic jerking, then a short coma
AbsenceLoss of consciousness; movement limited to blinking, head turns, eye movements
Epilepsy is the diagnosis for recurring seizures.
