? According to the textbook, how do the two theories of color vision fit together?
+ Both are correct at different levels: trichromatic at the retina's cones, opponent process at higher levels of visual analysis
- Trichromatic theory replaced opponent process theory after the 1917 Ishihara test
- Opponent process theory fits the cones, and trichromatic theory fits higher visual analysis
- Neither theory is supported; color comes from "grandmother cells"
@ Both theories are correct at different levels

? Which statement about pitch theories is correct?
+ Place theory works well above 4,000 Hz; below 4,000 Hz the firing pattern matches the frequency (temporal theory)
- Temporal theory works well above 4,000 Hz; place theory works below 4,000 Hz
- High tones peak near the wide, floppy apex of the basilar membrane
- Low tones peak near the narrow, stiff base of the basilar membrane
@ Below 4,000 Hz, the pattern of neural firing matches the frequency of the sound

? How does the brain localize a sound horizontally?

- By measuring the sound's frequency on the basilar membrane
- By counting how many hair cells respond
@ horizontally we compare arrival times at each ear
