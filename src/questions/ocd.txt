- A repetitive, ritualistic behavior
- A false perception

? What is a compulsion?
+ A repetitive, ritualistic behavior associated with high anxiety
- An intrusive, distressing thought
- A fear of open spaces
- A sudden period of intense fear
@ Repetitive, ritualistic behavior associated with high anxiety

@ compulsions are efforts to ward off the anxiety produced by obsessions
