? How does SAMHSA describe individual trauma?
+ An event or circumstance resulting in physical harm, emotional harm, and/or life-threatening harm
- Any stressful day at school or work
- A mental disorder diagnosed after one month
- A disagreement with a friend
@ an event or circumstance resulting in physical harm, emotional harm, and/or life-threatening harm

? What is trauma-informed care?
+ A way to create safer environments for people who have experienced trauma
- A medication for PTSD
- A crisis line for disaster survivors
- A type of physical therapy
@ A way to create safer environments for people who have experienced trauma

? Which is one of the four R's of a trauma-informed approach?
+ Resists retraumatization
- Restrains clients
- Reports all trauma to police
- Refers everyone to inpatient care
@ Resists retraumatization | Actively avoids re-traumatizing people

? Which of the four R's means fully integrating knowledge about trauma into policies, procedures, and practices?
+ Responds
- Realizes
- Recognizes

@ Responds | By fully integrating knowledge about trauma into policies, procedures, and practices

? Which trauma-informed principle means participants and staff feel physically and psychologically safe?
+ Safety
- Trustworthiness and transparency
- Collaboration and mutuality
- Empowerment, voice, and choice
@ Safety | Participants and staff feel physically and psychologically safe

? What is seclusion?
+ The involuntary, solitary confinement of an individual
- Any device that limits movement
- A support group for trauma survivors
- Voluntary time alone to relax
@ The involuntary, solitary confinement of an individual

? What is restraint?
+ Any method, device, material, or drug that immobilizes or reduces a person's ability to move freely
- Keeping a person alone in a locked room
- Refusing to give a person medication
- Talking a person through a crisis
@ Any method, device, material, or drug that immobilizes

? What is child traumatic stress?

- A normal bad day for a child
- A learning disability
- Stress felt only by parents
@ Occurs when a child (0–18 years) feels intensely threatened

? Which is a proven treatment for child trauma?
+ Trauma-focused cognitive behavioral therapy
- Seclusion
- Psychoanalysis
- Ignoring the event
@ Trauma-focused cognitive behavioral therapy is a proven treatment for child trauma

- Rebuilding homes after a disaster
