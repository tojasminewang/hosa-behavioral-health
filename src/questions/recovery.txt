? How does SAMHSA define recovery?
+ A process of change through which individuals improve their health and wellness, live self-directed lives, and strive to reach their full potential
- Never using any substance again after one treatment
- A cure that removes all symptoms permanently
- A single event that happens when treatment ends
@ A process of change through which individuals improve their health and wellness

? According to SAMHSA, what is the foundation of recovery?
+ Hope
- Medication
- Money
- Willpower alone
@ **Hope**, the belief that these challenges and conditions can be overcome, is the foundation of recovery

? What are peer support workers?

- Licensed psychiatrists who prescribe medication
- Police officers trained in crisis response
- Family members who pay for treatment
@ People who have been successful in the recovery process who help others experiencing similar situations

+ Many pathways, such as clinical treatment, medications, faith-based approaches, and peer support
- Only one pathway: medication
- Only one pathway: abstinence-only support groups

@ Recovery is highly personal and occurs via **many pathways**
