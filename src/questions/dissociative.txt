? What characterizes a dissociative disorder?
+ Disruptions in a person's identity, memory, or consciousness
- Hallucinations and delusions
- Extreme mood swings between mania and depression
- Repetitive thoughts and rituals
@ A disorder characterized by disruptions in a person's identity, memory, or consciousness

? Forgetting important information about specific events, often after a traumatic event, is what?
+ Dissociative amnesia
- Dissociative fugue
- Anterograde amnesia
- Depersonalization/derealization disorder
@ Forgetting important information about specific events

? Confusion over one's identity combined with sudden travel and assuming a new identity is what?
+ Dissociative fugue
- Dissociative amnesia
- Dissociative identity disorder
- Somatic symptom disorder
@ Confusion over one's identity, often combined with sudden travel

? What was dissociative identity disorder previously called?
+ Multiple personality disorder
- Split personality schizophrenia

? Feeling as if you are watching yourself from outside your body while knowing the feeling is unrealistic describes what?
+ Depersonalization/derealization disorder
- Dissociative fugue
- A hallucination
- A delusion of control
@ Strong feelings of unreality about the self or the surrounding environment

? What is somatic symptom disorder?
+ Having chronic body symptoms about which a person is excessively concerned, preoccupied, or fearful
- Forgetting one's identity and traveling far away
- Fear of gaining weight
- Believing body parts are flawed
@ Having one or more chronic somatic (body) symptoms

? How does NAMI describe dissociative disorders?
+ An involuntary escape from reality, with a disconnection between thoughts, identity, consciousness and memory
- Repeated panic attacks that strike without warning
- Extreme highs and lows in mood and energy
- An intense fear of gaining weight
@ an involuntary escape from reality, with a disconnection between thoughts, identity, consciousness and memory

? What was dissociative identity disorder formerly called?
+ Multiple personality disorder
- Schizophrenia
- Borderline personality disorder
- Depersonalization disorder
@ Formerly called multiple personality disorder

? Which describes derealization?
+ Feeling that the people and things in the world around you are unreal
- Feeling detached from your own actions, as if watching a movie of yourself
- Being unable to remember important personal information
- Switching between multiple identities
@ Feeling that the people and things in the world around you are unreal

? Why do critics question some treatments for dissociative identity disorder?
+ Hypnosis used to "work through" memories is not supported by research and may create false memories
- Medication for it has been banned
- It is always cured by a single therapy session
- Therapists refuse to treat it
@ often using hypnosis, which is not supported by research and may create false memories

- They were diagnosed mainly in Asia
- They have never changed
@ diagnoses spiked in the mid-1990s after media attention
