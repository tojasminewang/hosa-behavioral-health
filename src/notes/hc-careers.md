## Overview
- Workforce, careers, laws, regulations, and guidelines are 5% of the written test

## Five careers compared
| Professional | Licence | Training | Care |
|---|---|---|---|
| Psychiatrist | Medical doctor (MD) | Bachelor's, MD, 5 years of residency, fellowship to subspecialize | Medical branch preventing, diagnosing, treating complex, serious mental, behavioural, emotional disorders |
| Clinical psychologist | PhD or PsyD | Bachelor's, master's, a PhD in clinical psychology, 5 years of work and practice to register with the College of Psychologists | Talk therapy and psychometric tests to diagnose and treat |
| Counselor | PhD, PsyD, doctor of education (EdD), or master's | Bachelor's, master's, CCPA certification | Short-term therapy for one specific problem (e.g., addiction, stress) |
| Hypnotherapist | American College of Hypnotherapy (ACH) licence | Bachelor's or master's, NBCCH or ACH certification | Treatment producing changes in sensations, perceptions, thoughts, or behaviours |
| Life coach | Certification programs | Training varies | Clarifying goals, finding obstacles, planning strategies for growth |
